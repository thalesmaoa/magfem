Guide
Open MagFEM ↗
📐

Parametric CAD

Lines, arcs and circles with constraints, dimensions bound to variables, trim, offset, mirror and patterns. Imports DXF, SVG and FEMM .fem files.

🧲

Magnetostatic, AC and transient

Planar or axisymmetric, nonlinear materials (B-H curves), magnets, eddy currents and strong coupling with an external circuit.

📊

Results

Field maps, flux lines, plots along lines, inductance, force and torque (Maxwell stress tensor), losses and animations.

🐍

Scripting

Every action becomes a console command. The local bridge connects Python, Matlab, Julia or any HTTP-capable language to the model open in the browser.
